## Purpose

This repository defines a Proof Protocol mapping between the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications and Proof Protocol evidence, efficacy, and proof semantics.

The OWASP risk categories are treated as pluggable inputs describing what may be tested. Proof Protocol remains framework-agnostic and independently establishes whether a selected control performed as claimed.

## Architectural principle

> **Threat frameworks are pluggable inputs to Proof Protocol. Proof Protocol is framework-agnostic.**

External frameworks identify what may be tested. Proof Protocol independently establishes whether a control worked and what evidence proves the result.
